# What's new in GLYPH

Downloads are in [Releases](https://github.com/Mqr1oo/GLYPH/releases). The app updates
itself once it is installed, and GLYPH updates itself over Wi-Fi from **Control › Firmware**.

---

## App 1.3 · Firmware 04.10.26 · October 2026

### 🗺️ Maps
- **A detailed dark map of the whole world**: streets, hiking trails, forest tracks, peaks with
  their height, rivers, lakes, huts and borders, easy on the eyes at night.
- **Save area**: keep what you see on screen for use with no signal.
- **Save for this route**: keep a 2 km band along your route, in one tap.
- With no signal you still get a world map with towns, forests and borders, and every place you
  have already looked at.
- Two looks: the dark map and satellite.
- **Find places worldwide, offline**: every town and village with 500 people or more is built
  into the app. Peaks, huts and streets are found online.

### 🧭 Routes
- **Bike and walking routes stay on land**: they no longer cross the sea by ferry when there is
  a way around.
- A smaller route panel that leaves the map in view. Once a route is found, the form folds into
  one line.
- **Send to GLYPH** now loads the route into the device's **internal memory**: no memory card
  needed. The small card button saves it to the memory card only.

### 📟 Firmware 04.10.26
- Routes can live in GLYPH's own memory (up to 40 KB, about 1,500 points). The route list shows
  them as **MEM**.

### 🔌 Setup
- **Install firmware over USB-C, from the phone.** It is the app's first screen: plug GLYPH into
  your phone, enter the access code that came with it, and tap Install. No computer, no internet.
  Also under **Control › App**.

### ✨ Look and feel
- Comms: Public and Secure get their own row, with icons, and a clearer empty chat.
- Control: one line says when GLYPH is not connected, instead of a label on every section.
- The guided tour always keeps its card on screen.
- The map credits fold into a small **i** in the corner.

## App 1.2 · Firmware 03.10.26 · October 2026

- **Encrypted Bluetooth with a pairing code**: one phone at a time, and only yours.
- **Set GLYPH up from the phone**: name, language, radio band and time zone.
- **Updates over the air**: the app gets new features without a new download.
- **Live trip notification** on Android, with distance and time while you record.
- A clock that works before the first GPS fix.
- New SOS and sleep screens, fewer screen refreshes and a lower power draw.
- Bluetooth stays up through standby. LoRa keeps listening.

## App 1.1 · Firmware 01.10.26 – 03.10.26.2 · October 2026

- **The Android app**: native Bluetooth, notifications and saving files. Works with no internet.
- A new iPhone-style design with a floating tab bar.
- **Books**: send novels to GLYPH and read them on the e-ink screen.
- **Updates over Wi-Fi**: GLYPH downloads new firmware itself, checks it, and keeps the old
  firmware if anything goes wrong.
- Save map areas for offline use.
- Route fit, route list and a clearer battery warning on the device.

## Firmware up to 07.09.26 · August – September 2026

- **Mesh**: messages hop through other units, up to 3 relays, and are marked delivered once a
  teammate's unit confirms them.
- The team channel moves to AES-256-GCM.
- A hardware health check: the device says which module does not answer.

## First release · April 2026

- E-ink tactical map with GPS breadcrumbs and KML routes on the memory card.
- Encrypted LoRa messenger, team radar for 5 teammates, SOS.
- Sensor screen, three power modes, 11 languages and time zones.
